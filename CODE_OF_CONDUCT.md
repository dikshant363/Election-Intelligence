# Election Intelligence Platform Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, or expression, level of experience, national origin, political views, or spiritual beliefs.

We will not tolerate harassment or discrimination of any kind.

## Our Standards
- Use welcoming, inclusive language.
- Be respectful and considerate of differing viewpoints.
- Assume positive intent.
- Step aside if you are uncomfortable.

## Enforcement
Incidents of abusive behavior will be addressed by the project team. Violations may result in removal from the project and community.

## Contact
For concerns, please contact the project maintainer at dikshant@example.com.
